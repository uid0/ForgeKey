# People-counter BOM

| Qty | Item | Minimum requirements | Notes |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense | ESP32-S3, PSRAM, integrated camera connector/module | Firmware target `seeed_xiao_esp32s3`. |
| 1 | OV3660 camera module | Factory Sense module | Included with XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense kit. |

| 1 | USB-C cable or 5 V harness | 5 V supply sized for WiFi transmit current | Use strain relief for permanent installs. |
| 1 | Mounting bracket | Rigid, repeatable camera aim | Mark field of view during commissioning. |
